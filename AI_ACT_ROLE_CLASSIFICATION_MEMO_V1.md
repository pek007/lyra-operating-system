## Purpose
Initial classification memo for EU AI Act role and risk assumptions.

## Current assumption (to be validated)
- System role: likely deployer for internal assistant use, provider risk to be evaluated if externalized/offered to third parties.
- Risk tier: currently not classified as Annex III high-risk; requires periodic re-check when use cases change.

## Trigger for reclassification
- New external users/clients onboarded
- Use cases entering regulated decision domains
- Material capability changes in autonomous actions

## Review cadence
Quarterly or upon trigger event.
