# PXS Wiki Domain Map

Date: 2026-04-03
Owner: Lyra / PX Strategy context
Status: Draft domain map

## Purpose
Define the first top-level domain structure for the PXS wiki as the human-facing compiled-wiki representation of PX Strategy business/domain knowledge.

This map is for the **PXS wiki**, not the Lyra wiki.
The PXS wiki should focus on business, offering, market, product, delivery, and domain intelligence relevant to PX Strategy.

---

## Core design rule
The PXS wiki should represent:
- reusable PXS business/domain knowledge
- offer and product knowledge
- market and category understanding
- delivery-relevant patterns
- client/account intelligence where appropriate and safely scoped

It should not become:
- the Lyra platform/capability wiki
- a dump of all raw reports
- a duplicate of operational truth surfaces such as plans, tasks, or canonical job state

---

## Proposed top-level domains

### 1. Business Units
Purpose:
- represent the major PXS business units, their knowledge domains, and cross-BU patterns

### 2. Offerings & Services
Purpose:
- represent offers, service models, packages, and delivery logic

### 3. Markets & Themes
Purpose:
- represent industries, market themes, strategic topics, and recurring business intelligence domains

### 4. Products & Tools
Purpose:
- represent PXS product/tool knowledge, including PXS Tools where relevant to PXS business value

### 5. Clients & Accounts
Purpose:
- represent reusable client/account intelligence where appropriate and safely scoped

### 6. Delivery Patterns
Purpose:
- represent repeatable delivery methods, commercial patterns, proposal patterns, and engagement structures

### 7. Research & Intelligence
Purpose:
- represent reusable research syntheses, comparison pages, and knowledge structures supporting PX Strategy work

---

## First likely wiki anchors
- PXS Tools
- AI / High Tech
- key offer/service pages
- strategic market themes
- delivery pattern pages

## Bottom line
The PXS wiki should be the compiled, human-browseable layer for PX Strategy business/domain intelligence, clearly separate from the Lyra platform wiki.
