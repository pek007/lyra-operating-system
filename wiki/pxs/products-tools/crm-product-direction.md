# CRM Product Direction

Status: draft wiki page
Date: 2026-04-03
Domain: Products & Tools

## Summary
CRM product direction represents the emerging product/tool effort to build CRM capability for PX Strategy in a way that supports internal commercial workflows and may later contribute to broader reusable product value.

## Why it matters
CRM is the first concrete product/tool effort now explicitly routed into PXS Tools. That makes it a practical anchor for how PXS Tools should operate as a business-side tool/product unit.

## Current understanding
The current direction is:
- CRM process ownership belongs with Sales & Client Relationship Management
- CRM product/tool development belongs with PXS Tools
- PXS OS should not remain the default home for this product-building work
- the CRM effort should also inform broader build-vs-integrate and architecture learning

## Open questions
- how broad should the CRM product effort become before it is treated as its own product line?
- what should remain PX Strategy-internal versus potentially externalizable later?
- what architecture and integration decisions will best support both internal value and future product potential?
