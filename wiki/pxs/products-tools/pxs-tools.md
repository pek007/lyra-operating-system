# PXS Tools

Status: draft wiki page
Date: 2026-04-03
Domain: Products & Tools

## Summary
PXS Tools is the PX Strategy business unit focused on building and evolving the tool suite that supports PX Strategy’s work and may later become client-facing products or SaaS offerings.

## Why it matters
PXS Tools is important because it turns recurring internal needs into reusable tools and potential product assets. It is the business-side expression of tool/product development inside PX Strategy.

## Current understanding
The current PXS Tools direction is:
- support PX Strategy business units with useful tools
- treat internal tools as potential strategic product seeds
- route CRM product development into this BU rather than leaving it inside PXS OS
- research both what to build and what to integrate
- include software/build/architecture best-practice learning as part of the BU’s work

## Key boundaries
- PXS Tools is not the same as the Lyra-level Knowledge Compilation capability
- PXS Tools owns PXS-relevant tool/product work
- the broader Knowledge Compilation capability belongs at the Lyra level
- PXS OS retains internal operating-system backbone responsibilities rather than broad product/tool ownership

## Current implications
PXS Tools is a strong candidate for an early PXS wiki anchor because it sits at the intersection of:
- internal tool building
- product thinking
- AI/tool landscape research
- future SaaS/product potential
