## What this wiki is for
Use this wiki to understand:
- what Lyra knows in reusable form
- how key concepts relate to each other
- what parts of the system are becoming more mature
- where important boundaries and patterns live

## What this wiki is not
This wiki is not:
- the canonical system of record for operational truth
- a dump of all notes and logs
- the PXS business/domain wiki

## Current growth focus
Current focus is to:
1. deepen the developed domains
2. keep internal linking and page quality coherent
3. expand only where the next page clearly improves navigation or understanding

## Important boundary
This wiki supports understanding and reuse. It is not the canonical system of record for operational truth, plans, or governance commitments.
