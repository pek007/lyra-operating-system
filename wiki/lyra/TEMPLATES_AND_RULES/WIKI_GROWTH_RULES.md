# Wiki Growth Rules

Status: draft
Date: 2026-04-03

## Purpose
Define the minimum rules for how the Lyra wiki should grow.

## Rule 1 — Wiki pages are durable browseable nodes
A wiki page should exist because it improves navigation, understanding, reuse, or clarity — not merely because a note was created.

## Rule 2 — Do not duplicate operational truth
The wiki may summarize or link to operational truth, but it should not quietly become the source of record for plans, priorities, risks, decisions, or task state.

## Rule 3 — Prefer living pages over proliferating one-off pages
When a concept or topic is recurring, improve the existing wiki page before creating a new parallel page.

## Rule 4 — Keep cross-links explicit
Pages should link to related pages where that helps navigation and understanding.

## Rule 5 — Page type should be explicit
A page should clearly behave as one of:
- concept page
- topic page
- capability page
- control pattern page
- other explicitly justified wiki page type

## Rule 6 — Notes are not automatically wiki pages
Raw captures, logs, and dated notes may feed the wiki, but they are not themselves automatically part of the wiki layer.

## Rule 7 — Mark uncertainty honestly
A page existing does not prove the underlying concept is mature or strongly evidenced.

## Rule 8 — Keep pages compact and structured
A good wiki page should be easy to scan, easy to reuse, and easy to maintain.

## Rule 9 — Use the wiki to make knowledge visible
The wiki should help a human answer:
- what do we know?
- what is missing?
- what connects to what?

## Rule 10 — Prefer disciplined accretion over messy completeness
A smaller coherent wiki is better than a large, weakly connected one.
