# A-005 — Vision

Status: Active draft v1
Owner: Lyra
Last updated: 2026-03-08

## Mission
Make continuous improvement a real operating capability across the product portfolio: always-on, evidence-backed, low-drag, and safe.

## Customers
Primary customers:
- Product owners running their own products
- Peter as portfolio sponsor and decision-maker

Secondary customers:
- End users of PX Strategy capabilities, who benefit from faster learning and fewer repeated mistakes
- Future operators of PXS who need a reusable improvement system, not ad hoc heroics

## Problems / Jobs
1. Improvement work risks becoming irregular, vague, or personality-dependent.
2. Repeated friction is not consistently converted into process changes, controls, or backlog items.
3. Cross-product learning can get lost when each product improves locally without a shared process.
4. Deployment of improvement mechanisms into PXS currently relies on an interim lane and needs stronger verification and migration discipline.

## Value Proposition
A-005 gives the portfolio a common improvement operating system:
- a clear process for finding, prioritizing, testing, and institutionalizing improvements
- a lightweight governance layer that keeps changes reversible by default
- a deployment path that allows PXS to consume improvement capabilities safely
- visibility so recurring failures become portfolio learning instead of repeated waste

## Non-goals
- Taking over day-to-day ownership of other products
- Forcing centralized approval for all local improvements
- Autonomous one-way-door changes to authority, security boundaries, credentials, or external integrations
- Improvement theater without evidence, owners, or review dates

## Success Definition (qualitative)
The product is successful when:
- every active product can run the same basic improvement loop locally
- important recurring friction is captured and converted into action within a predictable cadence
- PXS consumes the improvement assembly through a managed deployment path with verification evidence
- Peter gets signal on meaningful changes without needing to micromanage routine improvement work
- the portfolio shows fewer repeated misses and better follow-through over time
