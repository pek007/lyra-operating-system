# A-005 — Goals

Status: Active v1
Owner: Lyra
Last updated: 2026-03-08

## Goal A-005-G1 — Establish the portfolio improvement operating system
- Outcome: All active products have a usable, shared improvement process with clear minimum artifacts and review cadence.
- Leading indicators:
  - A-005 management artifacts are fully defined and active
  - Improvement process + cadence checklist are published and referenced
  - Product-specific improvement logs exist for active products
- Lagging indicators:
  - Fewer repeated unaddressed failure patterns across products
  - Higher proportion of issues converted into owned actions
- Guardrails:
  - Keep process lightweight and reversible
  - Do not create approval bottlenecks for routine low-risk improvement work
- Owner job role: Improvement Product Owner
- Exit criteria: Improvement process is active, understandable, and adopted as the default way of working across the portfolio

## Goal A-005-G2 — Make improvement deployment to PXS reliable
- Outcome: The improvement assembly is verifiable in PXS and has an explicit migration path from interim-copy to pinned lane.
- Leading indicators:
  - A-005 verify cycle completed with evidence
  - Lockfile and dashboard reflect actual status
  - Migration plan to pinned lane documented with dependencies and target date
- Lagging indicators:
  - Reduced drift between Lyra OS source assembly and PXS-consumed assembly
  - Fewer manual sync ambiguities during updates
- Guardrails:
  - No hidden cross-domain coupling
  - Maintain traceability for every deployed assembly change
- Owner job role: Improvement Product Owner
- Exit criteria: A-005 is operating in PXS with evidence-backed verification and a credible pinned-lane migration path

## Goal A-005-G3 — Convert recurring friction into measurable improvement throughput
- Outcome: Important friction signals routinely become prioritized, linked, evidence-backed improvements.
- Leading indicators:
  - Weekly top 1-3 improvements captured
  - Each accepted improvement has owner, success signal, review date, and linked execution artifact
  - Monthly pattern review performed
- Lagging indicators:
  - Reduced recurrence of the same misses/blockers
  - Better flow and less rework in product operations
- Guardrails:
  - Evidence over opinion
  - Strategic or real-world-consequence changes are surfaced to Peter
- Owner job role: Improvement Product Owner
- Exit criteria: Improvement work shows a sustained rhythm and generates visible portfolio learning
