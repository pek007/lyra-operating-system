## Purpose
Control Panel exists to provide the operator-experience and steering plane of Lyra OS: a trusted control surface that makes the system observable, steerable, and easier to operate without tribal knowledge.

## Why this product matters
As the operating system grows, operators need a clear place to inspect state, understand risk, steer runtime behavior, and maintain coherence between the system’s execution reality and its explicit design authority. Control Panel is the product responsible for that operator-facing role.

## Scope
This product includes:
- control-panel runtime and service surfaces
- operator visibility and control mechanisms
- portfolio synthesis and steering surfaces
- model stewardship surfaces for observing, maintaining, and improving the Lyra OS Model
- related policy/runbook guidance
- future boundary/interface work for broader product usage

This product does not own all underlying work state, policy substance, or detailed domain artifacts; it owns the operator-facing surface and stewardship layer over those underlying structures.
