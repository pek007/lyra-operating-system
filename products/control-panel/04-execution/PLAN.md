# Current Plan

## Current objectives
1. Clarify and strengthen the control-surface role in the wider product portfolio.
2. Keep interfaces and runtime boundaries explicit.
3. Improve operator trust in visibility and control mechanisms.
4. Operationalize Control Panel’s stewardship role for the Lyra OS Model.

## Current workstreams
### Workstream 1: Control-surface role clarity
- keep the product boundary explicit as Control Panel scope expands
- maintain distinction between operator-facing stewardship and detailed underlying domain ownership

### Workstream 2: Model stewardship operationalization
- establish the Lyra OS Model front door and change protocol as usable operating artifacts
- integrate Model stewardship into Control Panel planning and review
- identify where runtime/product/workspace reality appears misaligned with the Model

### Workstream 3: Cross-system steering and coherence
- improve Control Panel’s role in connecting runtime visibility, portfolio synthesis, and explicit design authority
- tighten the path from observed drift or friction into model-change proposals and review
