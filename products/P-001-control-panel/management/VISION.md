# Control Panel — Vision

## Mission
Make Lyra OS observable, governable, and continuously improvable through one trusted control surface.

## Customers
- Primary: Lyra OS Product Owners and operators
- Secondary: PXS-facing product teams consuming OS capabilities

## Problems/Jobs
- Need reliable visibility into state, risk, and change impact
- Need clear control points for safe operations and improvement

## Value Proposition
Fast, trustworthy operational decisions with strong traceability and low overhead.

## Non-goals
- Replacing all product-specific execution tooling
- Becoming a monolithic workflow system

## Success Definition (qualitative)
Control Panel is the default place to understand system state, decide next action, and verify outcomes.
