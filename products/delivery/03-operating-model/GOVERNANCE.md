# Governance

## Governance posture
Delivery operates with medium governance intensity: enough structure to protect quality, traceability, and release safety without turning software delivery into ceremony.

## Core controls
- evidence-backed completion
- explicit acceptance and verification expectations
- escalation for strategic delivery-model shifts, launches, and real-world consequence decisions
- product-level review of delivery health and recurring failure modes

## Escalation triggers
Escalate to Peter for:
- strategic delivery-model changes
- launches or broadly consequential releases
- decisions with meaningful real-world downside

## Review model
- weekly delivery review
- milestone review for major release-readiness decisions
- recurring improvement review for delivery-system friction
