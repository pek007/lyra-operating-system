## Priority 1
**Title:** Clarify product and process ownership boundaries
**Why this matters now:** Governance only helps the system evolve safely if ownership is explicit enough to prevent overlap, ambiguity, and accidental centralization.
**Current status:** Active and central to the current governance agenda.
**Next concrete step:** Finish tightening ownership rules and remove remaining ambiguous boundary surfaces between Governance, products, and shared coordination.
**Links:** `products/governance/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/governance/01-identity/VISION.md`, `products/governance/07-decisions/DECISIONS.md`

## Priority 2
**Title:** Reduce overlap between Governance, Security, and shared coordination artifacts
**Why this matters now:** Overlap creates duplicated authority, slower decisions, and drift toward a confusing central layer.
**Current status:** Known issue area; partial clarification exists but consolidation is still needed.
**Next concrete step:** Identify the remaining overlapping artifacts and decide what belongs in Governance versus Security versus shared coordination.
**Links:** `products/governance/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/security/06-architecture/BOUNDARY.md`, `products/governance/06-architecture/INTERFACES.md`

## Priority 3
**Title:** Keep change-control and rule authority explicit as Lyra OS grows
**Why this matters now:** More products, workspaces, and coordination mechanisms increase the risk of rules becoming implicit or contradictory.
**Current status:** Direction is clear, but the pressure increases as the operating system becomes more structured.
**Next concrete step:** Keep governance front-door artifacts and decision authority current when new cross-product mechanisms are introduced.
