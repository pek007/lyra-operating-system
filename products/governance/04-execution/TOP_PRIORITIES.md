Product: Governance
Last updated: 2026-03-14

## Priority 1
**Title:** Operate Governance as a real product with explicit outcomes and reviewable progress
**Why this matters now:** Governance should model the same explicit goal/evidence/priority discipline it expects from the rest of the portfolio.
**Current status:** Governance is substantively real, but still needs to keep its product activation and review posture intentionally sharp as the system grows.
**Next concrete step:** keep Governance priorities, plan, metrics, and review outputs explicitly tied to real product outcomes rather than allowing governance work to dissolve into ambient system maintenance.
**Links:** `products/governance/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/governance/05-performance/METRICS.md`, `products/governance/07-decisions/DECISIONS.md`

## Priority 2
**Title:** Complete Governance assembly v0.1 into a clearly consumable and verifiable pack
**Why this matters now:** Governance only becomes transferable value downstream when the assembly is explicit enough to install, verify, review, and maintain without hidden assumptions.
**Current status:** The assembly scaffold is credible, but packaging completeness and verification semantics still deserve to be treated as active product work.
**Next concrete step:** make the governance assembly packaging decision explicit, keep verify aligned with that decision, and treat downstream consumability as a tracked product outcome.
**Links:** `products/governance/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `assemblies/governance-policy/v0.1/assembly.yaml`, `assemblies/governance-policy/v0.1/ACTIVATION.md`, `assemblies/governance-policy/v0.1/VERIFY.md`

## Priority 3
**Title:** Extend machine-checkable governance toward the assembly surface and source-of-truth integrity
**Why this matters now:** Governance already gains leverage from machine-checkable validation, so the next governance-quality gain is to reduce drift between product state, assembly state, and what the repo actually ships.
**Current status:** Validator-backed governance is a major strength, but assembly integrity/completeness is not yet fully part of that control surface.
**Next concrete step:** define the next machine-checkable path for assembly integrity/completeness and keep product/assembly/source-of-truth relationships explicitly linked in governance artifacts.
**Links:** `products/governance/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/governance/04-execution/RISKS.md`, `products/governance/06-architecture/INTERFACES.md`
