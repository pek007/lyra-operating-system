# Strategy

## Strategic objective
Keep system direction, decision rights, and boundary rules explicit enough that growth does not turn into ambiguity.

## Strategic choices
1. Focus Governance on rules, authority, and coherence.
2. Keep product-owned operating logic inside products.
3. Centralize only genuine cross-product coordination and system rules.
4. Model shared governance policy families explicitly before broad rollout, then distribute them through the lightest delivery mode that preserves local authority, consumer usability, and enforcement fit.
