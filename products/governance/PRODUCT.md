- Owner: `Lyra`
- Domain: `OS`
- Type: `Internal`
- Status: `Active`

## Purpose
Governance exists to define the system-level rules, decision rights, change-control logic, and boundary discipline that keep Lyra OS coherent as it evolves.

## Why this product matters
Without a clear governance product, the operating system drifts into implicit rules, inconsistent approvals, and weak architectural coherence. Governance is the layer that makes direction, control, and change discipline explicit.

## Scope
This product includes:
- system charter and decision-rights logic
- change-control and rollback rules
- product/portfolio boundary rules
- machine-checkable governance direction where useful
- shared coordination rules that do not belong inside a single product
