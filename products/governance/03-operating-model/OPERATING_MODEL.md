# Operating Model

Governance runs through explicit rules, periodic review of drift/ambiguity, and selective tightening of controls where ambiguity creates real downside. It should clarify product ownership boundaries rather than absorb them.

Material governance hygiene gaps — including proof-case retirement misses, protocol formalization gaps, repeated authority ambiguity, and shared-policy adoption drift — should be converted into canonical TDE-linked improvement work rather than remaining only in prose artifacts. Open Governance-origin improvement items should stay visible in recurring review until dispositioned or closed, and closure should require linked evidence plus explicit source-to-closure trace.
