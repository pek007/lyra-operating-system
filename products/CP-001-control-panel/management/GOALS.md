# Control Panel — Goals

## Goal CP-G1
- Outcome: Increase confidence and speed of operational decision-making.
- Leading indicators: Decision packets complete on first pass; fewer clarification loops.
- Lagging indicators: Lower incident recurrence tied to decision quality.
- Guardrails: No reduced safety/compliance traceability.
- Owner job role: Product Owner (Control Panel)
- Exit criteria: Consistent evidence-backed decisions across major change types.

## Goal CP-G2
- Outcome: Make improvement work visible and executable.
- Leading indicators: Improvement items moved to accepted changes with evidence.
- Lagging indicators: Reduced unresolved operational debt.
- Guardrails: WIP remains bounded.
- Owner job role: Product Owner (Control Panel)
- Exit criteria: Improvement loop is routine and auditable.
