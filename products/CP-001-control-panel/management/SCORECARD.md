# Control Panel — Scorecard

- Customer value signal: Product owners can create and maintain complete product management artifacts with low friction.
- Reliability/quality signal: Required sections are present and internally consistent.
- Flow signal: Time from detected product issue to accepted improvement change.
- Risk/compliance signal: Share of major changes with complete decision + evidence trace.
- Cost-efficiency signal: Operational overhead to keep product governance current.
- Memory capability signal: Share of important memory-bearing artifacts with explicit scope + activation path.
- Memory reliability signal: Retrieval success on priority memory queries and reduction in continuity-loss incidents.
