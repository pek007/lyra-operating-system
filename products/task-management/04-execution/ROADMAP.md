# Roadmap

## Purpose
Show the sequence of meaningful changes needed to make Task Management operationally strong and downstream-consumable.

## Horizon 1 — Foundation and clarity
Status: in progress

Objectives:
- establish the first working Product-as-Code model for Task Management
- make product boundaries and interfaces explicit
- align the product model with TDE operating and readiness artifacts

Key outcomes:
- canonical product model exists and is usable
- product strategy, operating model, and interface surface are explicit
- product work is easier to inspect and steer

## Horizon 2 — Operational reliability
Status: next

Objectives:
- improve confidence in TDE operational readiness
- clarify health signals and readiness assessment
- reduce ambiguity around active work, blockers, and evidence

Key outcomes:
- readiness questions can be answered from product artifacts and evidence
- recurring failure modes and friction points are visible and prioritized
- product health can be reviewed without reconstructing context manually

## Horizon 3 — Downstream adoption in `pxs`
Status: planned

Objectives:
- define the formal downstream consumption path for `pxs`
- reduce hidden workspace-specific assumptions
- validate real downstream use and learn from it

Key outcomes:
- `pxs` can consume the product through explicit interfaces
- adoption friction falls
- product design is informed by real consumer usage rather than internal theory

## Horizon 4 — Externalization readiness
Status: later

Objectives:
- shape the product for cleaner packaging and broader reuse
- decide whether capability-pack, service boundary, or another distribution form is most appropriate
- improve portability and commercialization readiness if warranted

Key outcomes:
- clearer externalization path
- more stable contracts and artifact discipline
- productization optionality without forcing premature packaging
