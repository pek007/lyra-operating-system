# Strategy

## Strategic objective
Make Task Management the reliable operating layer for task and decision execution inside Lyra OS and for downstream consuming workspaces.

## Strategic choices
1. Focus first on operational reliability, not feature breadth.
2. Treat TDE as the core product capability and improve around real usage.
3. Build explicit interfaces for downstream consumers instead of allowing hidden coupling.
4. Prefer evidence-backed deployment and adoption over conceptual completeness.
5. Design the product so it can later be externalized or commercialized if useful.

## What we are optimizing for
- reliable execution
- decision traceability
- downstream usability
- low-friction adoption
- governance proportionate to risk

## What we are not optimizing for yet
- broad product surface area
- sophisticated UX beyond what is needed for operational use
- generalized commercialization packaging before internal proof
- over-abstracted architecture

## Sources of advantage
- close integration with actual operating work
- ability to iterate quickly inside the workspace
- direct linkage between management artifacts, jobs, and runtime behavior
- potential to turn operating discipline into reusable capability

## Critical assumptions
- users will adopt task/decision tooling only if it clearly improves follow-through
- traceability matters enough to justify explicit structure
- the product can serve both internal operational needs and future externalizable patterns

## Current strategic risks
- product scope may sprawl across too many adjacent coordination problems
- downstream interfaces may remain implicit unless actively designed
- governance overhead may grow faster than product value if not kept disciplined
