# Vision

Task Management becomes the trusted execution backbone for PX Strategy: a product that makes tasks and decisions visible, traceable, governable, and reliably executable across human and AI work.

If this product succeeds:
- important work does not disappear into chat or ad hoc memory
- decisions are captured with enough structure to be acted on and audited later
- execution becomes more reliable without becoming bureaucratic
- downstream workspaces such as `pxs` can consume task/decision capability through clear, reusable interfaces
- the capability can later be externalized or productized without redesigning it from scratch

## Future state
The product should function as a control system, not just a task list. It should help answer:
- What matters now?
- What was decided, by whom, and why?
- What is blocked?
- What is next?
- What evidence exists that the work is done?

## Design intent
Task Management should raise execution quality while keeping operating friction low. The aim is disciplined flow, not process theater.
