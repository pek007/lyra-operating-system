## Priority 3
**Title:** Operationalize the minimum cross-system measurement-and-follow-up standard, starting with the overnight loop
**Why this matters now:** Improvement now explicitly owns the minimum standard for lightweight measurement and follow-up on recurring operational loops. **Current status:** This responsibility is now codified in `DECISIONS.md` (D-005), `OPERATING_MODEL.md`, and `PLAN.md`, and the overnight ledger has now completed three live cycles.
