## Priority 1
**Title:** Keep portfolio improvement loops active and visible
**Why this matters now:** Improvement only compounds if the loops are alive, inspectable, and visibly connected to actual system behavior.
**Current status:** Core standing objective.
**Next concrete step:** Ensure improvement findings keep producing visible follow-through, not just reports or isolated observations.
**Links:** `products/improvement/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/improvement/03-operating-model/OPERATING_MODEL.md`, `products/improvement/05-performance/METRICS.md`

## Priority 2
**Title:** Reduce repeated misses and recurring friction
**Why this matters now:** Repeated friction is the clearest signal that improvement mechanisms are not yet closing the loop strongly enough.
**Current status:** Active improvement target across the portfolio.
**Next concrete step:** Continue turning recurring misses into explicit corrective/preventive changes in the relevant product or shared layer.
**Links:** `products/improvement/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/improvement/07-decisions/DECISIONS.md`, `CLOSED_LOOP_IMPROVEMENT_MODEL_V1.md`

## Priority 3
**Title:** Improve the deployment path for improvement mechanisms into consuming environments
**Why this matters now:** Improvement has limited leverage if its outputs stay abstract and do not land cleanly in the workspaces and products that need them.
**Current status:** Strategic direction is clear; downstream deployment discipline still needs strengthening.
**Next concrete step:** Make the route from improvement insight to adopted downstream artifact or workflow more explicit and repeatable.
**Links:** `products/improvement/04-execution/PLAN.md`, `products/improvement/06-architecture/INTERFACES.md`, `products/improvement/02-strategy/STRATEGY.md`
