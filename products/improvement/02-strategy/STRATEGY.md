# Strategy

## Strategic objective
Make continuous improvement systematic, lightweight, and safe across the product portfolio without confusing it with the canonical execution engine itself, while also turning high-signal external AI-agent opportunities into disciplined internal evaluation and follow-through.

## Strategic choices
1. Focus on evidence-backed improvements.
2. Treat Improvement as the closed-loop learning layer, not the canonical task-state layer.
3. Make recurring friction visible and actionable.
4. Keep rollout reversible where possible.
5. Treat external AI-agent and OpenClaw opportunity scouting as a bounded upstream input to the improvement loop, not as trend collection for its own sake.
6. Prefer explicit watch/reject/test/adopt routing over repeated rediscovery of the same external ideas.
