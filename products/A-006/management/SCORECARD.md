# A-006 — Scorecard

Status: Active

- Customer value signal:
  - Primary measure: percentage of active Delivery work that results in a working, usable outcome for the requesting product/customer without major rework.
  - Baseline status: not yet measured systematically.
  - Near-term proxy: acceptance of Delivery outputs in-channel without reopening due to missing essentials.

- Reliability/quality signal:
  - Primary measure: share of completed Delivery work with explicit acceptance criteria met and completion evidence captured.
  - Baseline status: process assets exist, but adherence is not yet measured.
  - Near-term proxy: use of DoD, delivery gate, and evidence references in completed work.

- Flow signal:
  - Primary measure: time from accepted work start to verified completion for meaningful Delivery initiatives; secondary signal is work-in-progress kept at manageable levels.
  - Baseline status: no formal cycle-time baseline yet.
  - Near-term proxy: smaller batches, clearer Now/Next/Later decisions, and fewer stalled delivery items.

- Risk/compliance signal:
  - Primary measure: count of Delivery changes with unresolved security/architecture/release-readiness concerns at close.
  - Baseline status: not yet measured systematically.
  - Near-term proxy: explicit use of delivery gates, rollback thinking, and escalation on high-risk decisions.

- Cost-efficiency signal:
  - Primary measure: useful verified output produced per unit of management/implementation effort.
  - Baseline status: no direct cost telemetry yet.
  - Near-term proxy: reduced rework, reduced coordination overhead, and increased reuse of standard delivery assets.

## Notes
- Initial scorecard is intentionally lightweight until evidence collection becomes more systematic.
- Recommended future evolution: add DORA-aligned trend tracking, verification-debt tracking, and first-pass acceptance rate by work type.

## Baseline note added via delivery proof case
- Verification/operating-rhythm baseline: Delivery should maintain at least one explicit evidence-backed verification checkpoint per active improvement cycle.
- Current baseline state: expected by process, but not yet consistently evidenced as a recurring measured pattern.
- Immediate next target: capture one lightweight baseline artifact tied to the next VERIFY / operating-rhythm review cycle and treat that as the first concrete measurement anchor.
