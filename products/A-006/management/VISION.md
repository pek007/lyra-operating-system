# A-006 — Vision

Status: Active
Product Name: Delivery
Product Owner: Lyra

## Mission
Design, run, and continuously improve the Delivery product so Lyra OS can build, change, verify, and release capabilities safely, quickly, and with evidence.

## Customers
- Primary: Peter as the decision owner for product direction, trade-offs, and launch approval
- Primary internal: Lyra OS product owners who need a dependable way to get capabilities built and improved
- Secondary: Future operators of Lyra OS delivery tooling and development-management surfaces
- Secondary: PXS workspace as the current operating environment and first customer of the Delivery product

## Problems/Jobs
- Turn strategy and product needs into working, verified capabilities without avoidable chaos
- Provide a repeatable delivery system for both software changes and process/tooling changes
- Keep speed high without sacrificing security, architecture quality, or auditability
- Give product owners clear paths for intake, prioritization, implementation, verification, and release readiness
- Reduce verification debt, hidden work, and ad hoc execution across the workspace
- Build the internal delivery capability required for future product/tool development

## Value Proposition
Delivery gives Lyra OS a practical DevSecOps operating system: outcome-first planning, small-batch execution, explicit gates, evidence-backed completion, and continuous improvement of the delivery machine itself.

In practice, Delivery owns both:
- shipping capabilities
- improving how capabilities are shipped

## Non-goals
- Owning product strategy for all other products
- Replacing product owners' domain judgment on priorities or customer outcomes
- Optimizing for process ceremony over throughput and quality
- Launching customer-facing changes or making strategic delivery-model shifts without Peter's involvement
- Becoming a general-purpose reporting layer before the underlying delivery system is reliable

## Success Definition (qualitative)
Delivery is successful when Lyra OS can move from idea to safe working capability with low friction, clear ownership, and strong traceability; when changes are usually small, reversible, and well-verified; and when the delivery system itself improves continuously instead of accumulating invisible operational debt.
