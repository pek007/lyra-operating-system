# Risks

### R-001 — Security drift through scattered posture artifacts
- Description: Security posture can become fragmented across audits, evidence files, and governance notes.
- Consequence: Slow review, inconsistent interpretation, and accidental residual-risk drift.
- Mitigation: Maintain clear product-level posture and baseline artifacts.

### R-002 — Over-centralization
- Description: Security may drift into owning too much cross-product execution.
- Consequence: Bottlenecks, ambiguity, and weaker local ownership.
- Mitigation: Keep ownership, governance, and implementation boundaries explicit.

### R-003 — Under-enforced boundaries
- Description: Important trust-boundary or exposure changes may still depend too much on habit rather than strong controls.
- Consequence: Real downside with weak traceability.
- Mitigation: Keep escalation triggers explicit and improve machine-checkable assurance selectively.
