# Roadmap

## Horizon 1 — Posture clarity and boundary discipline
- keep security posture and residual-risk framing explicit
- make ownership boundary and deployment obligations easy to inspect
- preserve a clear product-level baseline for PXS security posture

## Horizon 2 — Recurring review and research conversion
- establish a lightweight standing review for research intake, posture drift, and control improvement
- turn security findings into explicit actions, decisions, or watch items

## Horizon 3 — More machine-checkable assurance
- automate selected control/evidence loops where they reduce ambiguity without creating excess noise
- strengthen posture verification while preserving operational usability

## Horizon 4 — Externalization readiness
- define what would be required for stronger packaging, tenant discipline, and broader customer applicability
