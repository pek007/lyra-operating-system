## 3. Main current gaps

### Gap A — Coverage breadth is incomplete
The wiki does not yet represent all major Lyra knowledge domains in a substantive way.

### Gap B — Coverage depth is uneven
Controls & Security is meaningfully deeper than most other domains.

### Gap C — Hubs are still thin
Top-level and domain hubs exist, but most are still skeletal and not yet rich browse surfaces.

### Gap D — Promotion logic is not yet routinized
We understand the loop conceptually, but the promotion path from research -> wiki is not yet a standing operating mechanism.

### Gap E — Nightly integration is weak
The wiki is not yet explicitly maintained through recurring nightly loops.

### Gap F — Self-improvement is limited
There is no standing stale-page / missing-page / promotion-candidate maintenance loop yet.

---

## 4. Priority gaps to address next

### Priority 1
Turn wiki maintenance into a recurring operating loop.

### Priority 2
Deepen one or two more domains rather than broadening shallowly everywhere.

### Priority 3
Create the first real cluster in one currently thin domain:
- Capabilities, or
- Operating Patterns, or
- Tools & Agents

### Priority 4
Create visible gap/promotion handling as part of recurring review.

---

## 5. Recommended next focus
Recommended next focus order:
1. operate the wiki maintenance loop
2. deepen Capabilities or Operating Patterns
3. add promotion/gap handling into recurring product/capability nightlies

---

## 6. Bottom line
The Lyra wiki is real and promising, but still early.

Current state is best described as:
- **strong direction**
- **partial coverage**
- **uneven depth**
- **not yet fully integrated into recurring maintenance and planning loops**

That means the next stage should be less about creating isolated new pages and more about making wiki growth, maintenance, and gap handling part of the operating system.
