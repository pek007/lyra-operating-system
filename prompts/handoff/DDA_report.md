# DDA_report.md
Version: 1.0.0
Artifact: Decision/Design Artifact (Research -> Decision)

## Required sections
1. Decision statement
2. Context and constraints
3. Options considered
4. Recommended option
5. Tradeoffs and risks
6. Implementation implications
7. Evidence/citations
8. Open questions
9. Decision owner and due date

## Quality bar
- recommendation must be explicit
- risks must include mitigation proposals
- citations required for non-obvious claims
